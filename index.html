FrostZone 3000 — Store #482, Line 2

Increase the next delivery by 18 cases and confirm shelf capacity with the store manager.

⏳ Inventory data last synced 6 hours ago — may not reflect this morning's deliveries.

Supporting evidence

  • Sales velocity increased 14% over the last two weeks.
  • Current on-hand inventory is estimated to last about 4 days.
  • A promotion for this product begins in 6 days.
  • A similar promotion increased demand 22% at three comparable stores.
  • Inventory count was last synced 6 hours ago and may be incomplete.
What is being recommended?
Increase the next scheduled delivery by 18 cases and confirm shelf capacity with the store manager before it ships.
Why is it recommended?
Sales velocity is rising ahead of a promotion, and current inventory is projected to run out before the promotion ends.
What data supports it?
Two weeks of point-of-sale velocity, current on-hand inventory, the promotion calendar, and outcomes from three comparable stores' prior promotions.
How current is the data?
Sales data is current as of today. Inventory data is 6 hours old and may not include this morning's deliveries.
How confident is the system?
Medium. The comparable-store pattern is directionally reliable but based on a small sample of three stores.
What assumptions were made?
That the promotion proceeds on schedule, and that this store's shoppers respond similarly to the three comparable stores used for the estimate.
What could make this recommendation wrong?
Undercounted stockroom inventory, a delayed or cancelled promotion, or a shelf that physically cannot hold 18 additional cases.
What happens if you approve?
A draft delivery order is created for manager sign-off. Nothing is submitted to the distribution system automatically.
Can this be edited or reversed?
Yes. The quantity below can be changed before approval, and an approved draft order can still be reversed by a manager before it is fulfilled.
